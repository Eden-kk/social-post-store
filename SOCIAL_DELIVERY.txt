Important limits:
Local three-JVM logical regions, not real regional deployment or high-QPS proof.
Views admit against local metadata snapshot: a stale region or concurrent local
delete may still admit telemetry. Known tombstone hides social reads, not global
physical erasure; old successful request retries return historical results.
Views use origin-local retained dedup; no 24h TTL/global exactly-once, and only
local durable ACK (permanent unique disk loss can lose accepted events).
Likes do not solve arbitrary multi-device order: a second writer is rejected.
No PENDING job API/MQ, OR-Set, auth, sharding, compaction, cross-store transaction,
one-minute RPO, or measured throughput claim. UNKNOWN means outcome uncertainty,
not durable queued work or cancellation. Headers/owner IDs are trusted demo input.
